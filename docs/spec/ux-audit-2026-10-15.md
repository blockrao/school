# UX and product audit, live site (15 Oct 2026)

Method: screenshots of the live home, Gurugram hub, a school page (G D Public School) and /admissions at 1360 px and 390 px, plus a read of the markup. Data is sample, so thin content is expected; this audit is about how the product behaves when data is thin and when it is rich.

## What already works
- Honest provenance language, no stars, no paid ranking (footer says so).
- Fast, image-free pages; no horizontal scroll on mobile; bottom tab bar on phones.
- Clear national home with city grid; city hub is a real hub; sticky section nav on school pages.
- "What SchoolOye knows / still being verified" panel is the right idea.

## Findings and fixes

P0 - trust and conversion defects
1. "Not yet verified" is printed where the fact is simply missing (Admissions, Annual fee, Student-teacher ratio, Board result, Board). Missing is not unverified. Use "Not reported" everywhere (same rule as the new Fees block).
2. "Ask this school" says "Sign in to ask this school a question". Contradicts the rule that contact is never login-gated. Replace with the open enquiry form.
3. Raw data leaks into the page: Address block shows "City: NA" and a street line that repeats the school name. Clean or hide when not meaningful.
4. Header header is cluttered: unclaimed notice, Save button, a large placeholder "GD" square, share, compare, claim link, all before any fact. First screen on a phone has no decision information.
5. "Is this your school? Claim it free" appears three times (header, right rail, knows-panel). Keep one prominent placement plus one in the footer of the page.
6. Header says "Class 1-7 · Other" - board "Other" is meaningless to a parent when the board is simply unknown. Show "Board not reported".

P1 - make thin pages useful, rich pages compelling
7. Thin-state design: when more than half the facts are missing, lead with a "What we know / what to ask the school" summary and the enquiry action, not a grid of empty cells.
8. Move the completeness panel to the top as a compact meter ("6 of 30 facts reported") with the provenance legend.
9. Key-facts strip with source under each number (students, ratio, established, fee) once data exists.
10. Replace the placeholder initials block with a small neutral initials mark, or the school's own logo once supplied.
11. Sticky bottom action bar on mobile (Enquire, Save, Alerts) so the primary action is always one tap away.
12. Admissions block per entry class (window, age rule, seats, selection, documents, alert CTA).

P1 - listing cards (hub and list)
13. Cards repeat "Not yet published" three times and a clipped "Dates not yet published" side column. Collapse all missing facts into one quiet line; show only what we have (short board name, grades, locality).
14. Use short board names (CBSE, ICSE) on cards, not "Central Board of Secondary Education".
15. Hub category list shows only "ICSE/ISC schools (7)" while the cards are CBSE: board counting from affiliations is incomplete for Gurugram (data fix), so the CBSE hub is missing.
16. Make the filter/sort bar visible on the list view with removable chips (Niche pattern).

P2 - home and discovery
17. Home is clear but plain: add three trust lines under search (every fact sourced and dated; no paid ranking; free for parents), quick-start chips (Nursery admissions, CBSE, near me), and a "how it works" row.
18. "Admissions open now" empty state on home is the first thing under the fold: show "closing soon / opening soon" or the alert signup instead.
19. Typeface: pages appear to render in a generic system sans rather than the brand typeface; confirm font loading and apply the type scale from the design mockup.

P2 - technology advantage (what competitors do not have)
20. Grounded "Ask about this school" answer box using only published facts, each answer citing its source line (AEO-native, no hallucination).
21. Admission alerts by WhatsApp/email per school and per class.
22. "What changed since last year" diff on fees and admission dates (we log every change).
23. Commute check and eligibility check in the browser, no data stored.
24. School self-serve portal: schools publish their own facts with documents, labelled "Provided by the school".
25. Shortlist and compare across fees, dates, board with shareable link for the other parent.
26. Hindi and regional-language pages with hreflang once English is solid.

P3 - measurement
27. Core Web Vitals field data, Search Console baseline, AI-citation query set, and funnel events (view school -> enquiry / alert signup).
